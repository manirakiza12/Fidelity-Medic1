About Us

A trusted, community-first healthcare centre in Uganda

Fidelity Medic was founded on a simple conviction: that quality healthcare should feel close to home. From our clinic in Kampala, we deliver accessible, evidence-based care to patients of every age and income — and we take that care out into the communities that need it most.

Our team of qualified clinicians, laboratory scientists and community health workers is united by one promise: where your life matters.

Reliability

Consistent, dependable care backed by qualified practitioners and modern protocols.

Accessibility

Affordable, welcoming care for every family — regardless of background or means.

High Standards

Strict hygiene, sterile protocols and diagnostics you can trust without question.

Community Impact

Beyond the clinic — care without borders

Care should not stop at our doorsteps. In partnership with local leaders, schools and community organisations, Fidelity Medic runs free medical camps, screening drives and health education programmes across Uganda.

Free health screenings

Blood pressure, sugar and malaria checks at zero cost.

Maternal & child health

Antenatal outreach and immunisation days for rural mothers.

Health education

Practical workshops on hygiene, nutrition and disease prevention.

Medicine donations

Essential drugs and supplies donated to vulnerable families.
